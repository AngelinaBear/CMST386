About Us

Our Mission

Happy Tails Dog Daycare & Boarding is dedicated to providing a safe, clean, and welcoming environment where dogs receive the attention, exercise, and care they deserve. Our goal is to give pet owners peace of mind by offering dependable daycare, overnight boarding, and grooming services performed by caring staff who treat every dog like part of the family.

Why Choose Happy Tails?

Located in Stafford, Virginia, Happy Tails serves local families, military members, and working professionals who need trusted pet care. We focus on creating a fun experience for every dog while maintaining high standards of cleanliness, safety, and personalized attention. We believe every visit should leave both pets and their owners happy and confident in our services.
