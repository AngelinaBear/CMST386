Overnight Boarding

Comfortable Care While You Are Away

Happy Tails provides overnight boarding for dogs whose owners are traveling or need dependable care away from home. Each dog receives regular meals, fresh water, supervised exercise, rest time, and attention from our staff throughout the stay.

What Boarding Includes

  • Clean and comfortable sleeping areas
  • Scheduled feeding based on owner instructions
  • Daily exercise and supervised playtime
  • Fresh water throughout the day and night
  • Regular attention from trained staff
